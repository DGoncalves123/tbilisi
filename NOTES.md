## Working context / framing (author's notes)
- NATO operations are mostly settled; Georgia is still seeking more "multilateralism" — moving into more EU institutions, toward more centralized management under what the author calls the "White empire" / US empire (final name TBD).
- The country has already liberalized a lot: predatory businesses opened, more land taken, more cults, more expats, more tourists arriving.
- Translation note: the author translates "soviet" as "council".

## Seen but not photographed — to be added as text
1. **Hare Krishnas** — singing in the morning in the street with the mural and the Coca-Cola logo (there IS a photo of this scene); "singing the same thing forever"; we just passed them.
2. **Flags** — UK flags and US flags on display.
3. **Anti-Russia signage** — English-language signs telling people not to like Russians / "Russians are bad".
4. **Expat scene** — the expat community is very anti-Russian; the digital-nomad / mass-tourism scene works like a "liberalizing insurgency".
5. **House removals** — the Tbilisi government is renovating; it removed people's houses in the center, taking them for free and selling to foreigners, while new expats blame everything on Russia.
6. **Parliament door (second night)** — blocked by a thin fence, looked closed/unusable; the circle symbol on top was removed (just a circle left in the door); two plywood ("contraplacado") panels blocking it; another wall after that blocked the street from the people in the inner part; the inner entrance was open on both sides, heavy police presence but fully calm, almost no one there; one photographer; old English people carrying a British flag.
7. **Trump Tower ad** — a giant advertisement for Trump Tower.
